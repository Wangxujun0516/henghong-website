## 🛠️ Technical Overview & Electrophoretic Surface Integrity

In the coastal RV camping and winter logistics sectors, traditional chassis components degrade rapidly due to severe salt-spray exposure and road grime abrasion. HENGHONG solves this industrial vulnerability by subjecting the entire SC-Series production run to a multi-layer **Automotive Black E-Coat (Electrophoretic Coating)** finish. This chemical bonding process guarantees 100% surface coverage—including internal threads and scissor linkages—delivering elite rust prevention that far outlasts standard liquid spray paints.

The mechanical actuation relies on a precision-machined, high-tensile threaded drive screw. This design provides smooth extension and self-locking capabilities under peak static loads. Designed with an **oversized, widened steel footprint base**, the jack minimizes localized ground pressure, preventing sinking and tilting on unstable surfaces like mud, sand, gravel, or wet grass.
