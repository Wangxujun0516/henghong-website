## Product Overview

**Premium Heavy-Duty RV Scissor Jacks & Caravan Stabilizers**

Henghong scissor jacks are engineered to provide maximum stability and dependable leveling for travel trailers, campers, and pop-up RVs. Built from high-tensile structural steel, these jacks deliver quick extension and heavy-weight support, eliminating vehicle swaying and rocking at the campsite.

## Key Engineering Features

- **High-Tensile Steel Construction:** Manufactured using heavy-gauge, structural-grade steel for maximum rigidity and resistance to bending under peak loads.
- **Anti-Corrosion Black E-Coat:** Finished with a premium, multi-layer black e-coating (electrophoretic coating) providing exceptional rust and weather protection, proven to withstand long-term outdoor exposure.
- **Smooth Rapid-Extension Mechanism:** Features a precision-machined threaded drive screw that provides effortless and smooth worm-gear extension using a standard crank handle or power drill adapter.
- **Widened Stabilizing Footprint:** Designed with an oversized, extra-wide steel base foot to ensure secure anchoring on soft terrain like dirt, mud, or gravel.
