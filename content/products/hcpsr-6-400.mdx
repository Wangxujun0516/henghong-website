# 12T Heavy-Duty Auto Electric Leveling System (Model: HCPSR-6)

## Heavy-Duty 4-Point Dynamic Stabilization System with Smart LCD Control Architecture

The **Model: HCPSR-6** is an industrial-grade, 4-point automatic electromechanical stabilizing and leveling matrix engineered specifically for Class A motorhomes, heavy-duty travel trailers, and commercial mobile platforms. Deployed as a fluid-free alternative to legacy hydraulic jacks, the HCPSR-6 combines precision planetary gearboxes, high-torque brushless DC motors, and a dual-axis MEMS inclinometer feedback loop to deliver rapid, drift-free stabilization on unpaved or uneven terrain.

* **Actuation Mechanism:** Pure Electromechanical (Worm-Gear + Acme Spindle)
* **Control Interface:** One-Touch Smart LCD Control + Wireless RF Remote + Manual Override
* **Global Compliance:** FCC, IC, CE Certified (Full Technical File Ready)
* **Supply Compatibility:** Dual-Voltage Self-Adaptive Architecture (DC 12V / DC 24V)

## 🛠️ Product Overview & Thermodynamic Edge

Unlike hydraulic stabilization systems that suffer from low-temperature fluid viscosity spikes (causing asymmetric chassis twist) and high-temperature seal degradation, the **HCPSR-6 Auto Electric Leveling System** operates entirely on a solid-state electromechanical column.

By eliminating hydraulic lines, central pumps, and fluid reservoirs, it completely removes the compliance burden under **EPA 40 CFR Part 279** and guarantees a {"<"}200 ms motor energization response time across an extreme thermal envelope of **−30°C to +70°C**. The integrated worm-gear assembly provides a mechanical self-locking hold force, eliminating the dynamic "creep" or leak-down common in failing hydraulic valve blocks.

## ⚡ Key Engineering Features & OEM Differentiators

* **Dynamic Load Mitigation Firmware:** The central control module constantly samples current draw from each individual jack. If a leg encounters soft soil or localized settlement, the algorithm modulates the voltage to equalize structural loading across the vehicle frame, preventing chassis warping (±0.1° precision tracking).
* **Dual-Voltage Harness Efficiency:** Native compatibility with DC 12V and DC 24V architectures allows coach-builders to streamline wiring loops. At 24V, current draw is halved, allowing a **35% reduction in copper wiring harness cross-section weight** and minimizing voltage drop during simultaneous 4-leg deployment.
* **Self-Locking Mechanical Safety Core:** Features a hardened inner steel spindle paired with a high-ratio worm drive. The system maintains positive mechanical locking under a static 10-ton load without continuous battery drain, satisfying **OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147** lockout requirements during field service.
* **Intertek Audited Manufacturing:** Produced in a 39,000 m² state-of-the-art facility backed by a 21-engineer R&D center. Every batch undergoes full structural load testing, ensuring 100% material traceability and compliance with the **EU Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC**.
