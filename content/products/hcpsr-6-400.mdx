## ⚡ Key Engineering Standards & Product Differentiators

* **Active Load Balancing Control:** The centralized microprocessor continuously monitors the electrical current across all four legs. If one jack encounters soft terrain or localized shifting, the control algorithm alters power distribution to stabilize the frame, keeping flat-plane tracking within **±0.1° precision** to protect the vehicle structure.
* **Mechanical Self-Locking Integrity:** The internal worm-gear drive mechanism functions as a dependable mechanical lock when power is switched off. The system safely supports static loads without drawing continuous power from the coach batteries, conforming to standard safety protocols during stationary vehicle use.
* **Automated Factory Load Testing:** Quality control is fully integrated into our production lines. Every HCPSR-6 leveling run undergoes non-destructive structural stress testing on automated beds, ensuring consistent material performance and reducing the risk of component failure upon arrival.
