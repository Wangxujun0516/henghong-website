# 3500 lbs Heavy-Duty Electric Tongue Jack (12V DC)

## Industrial-Grade A-Frame Hitch Jack for Travel Trailers & RVs

The **Heavy-Duty Electric Tongue Jack** is engineered to eliminate the physical strain of manual cranking, delivering effortless, push-button hitching and leveling for travel trailers, cargo haulers, and agricultural A-frame trailers. Built around a high-torque 12V DC motor and a heavy-gauge steel column, this jack provides a robust **3500 lbs (approx. 1587 kg)** lifting capacity.

Whether you are an RV parts distributor looking for reliable retail-ready stock or an OEM builder seeking certified components, this electric tongue jack combines rapid deployment, global regulatory compliance, and rugged weather resistance.
