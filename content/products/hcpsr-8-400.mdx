The **Model: HCPSR-8** is an advanced, high-capacity 4-point electromechanical stabilization system engineered for premium mid-to-heavy travel trailers, commercial specialty vehicles, and large-scale motorhome platforms. ## 🛠️ Product Overview & 24V Powertrain Superiority

Unlike entry-level 12V stabilization systems that suffer from massive voltage drops and excessive thermal expansion during heavy-duty concurrent lifts, the **HCPSR-8 Auto Electric Leveling System** operates natively on a **DC 24V bus**.

By doubling the operational voltage, the system cuts current draw in half under peak load, drastically mitigating thermal degradation within the central control box and wiring harness. This allows coach-builders to utilize lighter, higher-gauge wiring while ensuring a flawless, simultaneous 4-leg deployment speed. The system's ultra-stable **680 mm max height** combined with a full **400 mm extension stroke** provides an optimized slenderness ratio (L/r), delivering exceptional resistance against lateral wind loads and severe chassis sway on unpaved surfaces.

## ⚡ Key Engineering Features & OEM Differentiators

* **Intelligent Terrain Synchronization:** The central processing node utilizes an active MEMS inclinometer to sense localized ground settlement. The system automatically modulates the power distribution to individual jacks, ensuring a perfectly level cabin (±0.1° precision tracking) while eliminating structural stress on slide-out mechanisms.
* **Low-Profile Buckling Resistance:** With a maximum height of 680 mm and high-strength steel construction, the HCPSR-8 offers a significantly reduced structural overhang when retracted, drastically minimizing wind resistance and preventing structural damage during transit on rugged off-road terrains.
* **Pure Electromechanical Self-Locking Core:** Features a high-efficiency worm-gear assembly that creates an absolute mechanical hold force once deactivated. The system maintains positive locking under a static 8-ton load indefinitely without zero continuous current draw from the coach batteries, fully satisfying **OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147** safety protocols.
