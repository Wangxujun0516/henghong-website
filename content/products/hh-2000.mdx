# Heavy-Duty Industrial-Grade Electric Hydraulic Leveling System (Model: HH-2000)

## High-Pressure Synchronized Electro-Hydraulic Stabilization Matrix for Heavy Machinery & Caravans

The **Model: HH-2000** is a premium, high-pressure electro-hydraulic stabilization and lifting system manufactured by **HENGHONG** (Zhejiang, China). Engineered for both heavy-duty towing applications and precise industrial equipment orientation, the HH-2000 combines the digital responsiveness of an automated micro-processing control interface with the unyielding structural rigidity of high-pressure hydraulics. Deployed as a centralized lifting station with a total system capacity of **4 Ton (4,000 kg)**, its robust structural alloy columns allow individual fluid cylinders to sustain variable vertical loads from **1 Ton up to 10 Ton per leg**, perfectly handling severe offset weights and heavy asymmetric machinery footprints.

## 🛠️ Technical Overview & Structural Metallurgy

Weighing in at a heavy-duty **75 kg** for the complete mechanical assembly, the **HENGHONG HH-2000** system is purposely built to replace flimsy mechanical stabilizers. With an ultra-low minimum stowed profile of **350 mm** and extending up to a maximum height of **630 mm**, it preserves crucial ground clearance while maintaining excellent resistance against severe buckling or torsional bending moments when lifting heavy manufacturing blocks or expansive trailer frames.

By migrating the hydraulic pump into a self-contained, weather-sealed monoblock unit, this system removes the vulnerability of long, high-pressure external fluid run lines, guaranteeing rapid response times and zero mechanical backlash. Pilot-operated check valves lock the fluid within the carbon steel columns immediately upon target alignment, providing an absolute rigid base that removes suspension bounce during stationary operations.
