# 1000KG Heavy-Duty Electric Stabilizer Jack (Model: HH-400)

## 12V DC Electromechanical Chassis Stabilization Matrix | Engineered for RV Slide-Outs & Axle Support

The **Model: HH-400** is an industrial-grade, low-profile electromechanical stabilizer jack engineered by **HENGHONG** (Zhejiang, China). Purpose-built to eliminate the tedious labor of manual scissor cranking, this 12V DC power jack is optimized for lateral and side-to-side stabilization on touring caravans, travel trailers, and heavy mobile platforms.

Engineered for versatility, the HH-400 serves as a premier **turnkey solution for retrofitting and upgrading legacy manual chassis**. It delivers a rigid **1000 kg (2,204 lbs)** ultimate static holding capacity and a **540 kg (1,190 lbs)** rated dynamic lifting threshold, making it highly effective for trailer axle stabilization and preventing catastrophic frame tilting during heavy slide-out extensions.

## 🛠️ Technical Overview & Retrofit Engineering Edge

Traditional consumer-grade scissor jacks frequently suffer from lateral sway under wind loads and rapid screw thread binding when exposed to road salt. The **HENGHONG HH-400** directly mitigates these vulnerabilities by utilizing a reinforced steel link geometry paired with an enclosed, weather-sealed **100W electromechanical drive motor**.

The precision-machined internal lead screw is protected by a heavy-duty, marine-grade Black Powder Coating that shields the steel from corrosion and grit buildup. With a substantial **504 mm vertical travel stroke**, this stabilizer accommodates varied ground clearances without the need for unsafe wooden blocks. It provides a rock-solid, stable foundation that eliminates structural cabin rocking and prevents long-term stress on trailer slide-out seals and suspension bushings.
