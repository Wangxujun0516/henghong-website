## 🛠️ Technical Overview & Environmental Survivability

In rugged construction sites, remote farming sectors, and off-grid overland trails, electronic components can frequently become liabilities due to water submersion, mud clogging, or battery drain. The **HENGHONG Manual Steel Jack** solves this vulnerability by prioritizing high-tensile structural metallurgy over complex circuitry. The internal lift mechanism features a precision-machined, high-carbon steel vertical lead screw paired with heavy-duty reduction gears to reduce operating friction, allowing effortless manual deployment under peak load weights.

The entire outer housing and telescopic steel inner tube are treated with an anti-corrosion, marine-grade Industrial Powder Coating. This specialized coating creates a tight physical barrier against road-salt flaking, continuous UV peeling, and impact scratches from road debris. Fitted with a widened heavy-gauge steel foot pad, it effectively distributes vertical weight loading to prevent sinking on soft sand, deep mud, or loose gravel.
