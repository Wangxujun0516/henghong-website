## About Henghong Intelligent Equipment

Established in 2004, **Henghong Intelligent Equipment Co., Ltd.** is a national high-tech enterprise located in Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province, China. With a 39,000㎡ manufacturing facility and 139 employees (including 21 R&D engineers), we specialize in the design and production of high-precision **Automatic Leveling Systems**, Electric Jacks, and RV intelligent equipment.

As an **Intertek Verified Supplier**, we combine advanced servo control technology with robust mechanical engineering to deliver reliable solutions for the global RV and special vehicle industry. Our products are exported to over 40 countries, with strong presence in North America and Oceania.

- **22 years** of industry experience and **19 years** of export expertise

- **Vertically integrated** manufacturing with 7 production lines
- Strong customization capability for OEM/ODM projects
