## 1. Water Ingress Protection: The #1 Killer of Fleet Jacks

Water penetration causes **35–45%** of all electric jack warranty claims.

### Why Water Kills Jacks Fast

During transit in heavy downpours or outdoor storage:

- Moisture seeps into the motor housing
- Causes short circuits and switch failures
- Freezes gears, rendering the jack inoperable

### Budget vs. OEM-Grade Standards

- **Budget Suppliers:** Use cheap plastic casings with standard gap-seals
- **UV Degradation:** Budget seals break down rapidly under sun exposure
- **OEM-Grade Standard:** Demand **UV-stabilized materials** with integrated **rubber gaskets** at every joint

### Henghong's Weatherproof Solution

At **Henghong**, all electric tongue jacks feature:

- Specialized weather-resistant covers
- Sealed membrane switches
- Marine-grade gaskets

This ensures continuous operation even in coastal humidity and severe rainstorms.

> **Procurement Insight:** A gasket-sealed motor head with IP-rated protection is not a premium feature — it is a cost-of-quality requirement for any volume OEM program.

## 2. Gear Metallurgy: Column Strengths to Reduce Warranty Risks

Under heavy dynamic loads or uneven terrain, the internal gearbox bears the entire strain. If gears strip, the jack fails instantly.

### Force Transmission Path

### The Risk of Cutting Corners

Many factories use sintered powder metal or nylon gears to save costs:

- Brittle under sudden stress
- Prone to shearing
- Fail at **1,500–3,000** cycles under rated load

### The Spec That Protects Your Fleet

Insist on **hardened steel gears** that:

- Distribute torque efficiently
- Maintain lifting speed over time
- Exceed **25,000** cycles with {"<"}**5%** backlash growth
- Reduce operating noise

### Henghong's Gearbox Standard

Our gearboxes are constructed entirely of precision-machined, hardened steel. This mechanical integrity extends the motor's operational life and reduces your fleet's replacement cycle cost.

> **Engineering Note:** Through-hardened steel gears deliver **16x+** longer service life compared to powder-metal alternatives in accelerated life testing.

## 4. Corrosion Resistance: Protecting Brand Value Long-Term

A rusted jack tube within **6 months** of delivery severely damages your trailer brand's premium perception.

### Corrosion Accelerators

Road salt, gravel spray, and sea air quickly destroy basic wet paint finishes:

- Visible surface corrosion devalues your entire trailer
- Rust signals poor quality to end-users
- Damages resale value in the secondary market

### Henghong's Anti-Corrosion Process

Our surface treatments include:

- **Electrostatic powder coating** over zinc-plated inner tube
- Laboratory-tested to withstand **500 hours** of continuous salt spray
- Prevents rust and maintains factory finish for **2–3+** seasons

> **Brand Impact:** A jack that retains its factory finish reinforces your brand's quality positioning. One with rusted tubes devalues the entire trailer.
