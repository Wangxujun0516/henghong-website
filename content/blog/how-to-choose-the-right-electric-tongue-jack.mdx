## 1. Water Ingress Protection: The #1 Killer of Fleet Jacks

The most common field failure for electric jacks is water penetration. During transit in heavy downpours or outdoor storage, moisture seeps into the motor housing, causing short circuits, switch failures, and frozen gears.

- **The Industry Bottleneck:** Budget suppliers use cheap plastic casings with standard gap-seals that degrade rapidly under UV exposure.
- **The OEM-Grade Standard:** Look for housing engineered with **UV-stabilized materials** and integrated **rubber gaskets** at every joint.
- **How Henghong Solves This:** At **Henghong**, all our electric tongue jacks feature specialized weather-resistant covers, sealed membrane switches, and marine-grade gaskets. This ensures continuous operation even in coastal humidity and severe rainstorms.

> **Procurement Insight:** Water-related failures account for an estimated 35–45% of all electric jack warranty claims in the RV and trailer industry. A gasket-sealed motor head with IP-rated protection is not a premium feature — it is a cost-of-quality requirement for any volume OEM program.

## 2. Gear Metallurgy: Hardened Steel vs. Powder Metal

Under heavy dynamic loads or uneven terrain, the internal gearbox bears the entire strain. If the gears strip, the jack fails instantly.

<pre class="bg-gray-50 p-4 rounded-lg text-sm font-mono overflow-x-auto">[ Heavy-Duty Motor ] ──> [ Hardened Steel Gears ] ──> [ Precision Lead Screw ] ──> Smooth Lift (Zero Slip)</pre>

- **The Risk:** Many factories cut costs by using sintered powder metal or nylon gears. These are brittle and prone to shearing under sudden stress.
- **The Spec to Demand:** Insist on **hardened steel gears** that distribute torque efficiently.
- **The Henghong Standard:** Our gearboxes are constructed entirely of precision-machined, hardened steel. This mechanical integrity reduces operating noise, maintains lifting speed, and extends the motor's operational life.

> **Engineering Note:** In side-by-side accelerated life testing, powder-metal gears typically fail at 1,500–3,000 cycles under rated load, while through-hardened steel gears exceed 25,000 cycles with {"<"}5% backlash growth. The material choice directly determines your fleet's replacement cycle cost.

## 4. Corrosion Resistance: The 500-Hour Salt Spray Test

A rusted jack tube within 6 months of delivery severely damages the premium perception of your trailer brand. Road salt, gravel spray, and sea air will quickly destroy basic wet paint finishes.

- **The Spec to Verify:** Demand a minimum rating on the salt spray test.
- **Our Manufacturing Standard:** Henghong utilizes **electrostatic powder coating** over a zinc-plated inner tube. Our surface treatments are laboratory-tested to withstand up to **500 hours of continuous salt spray**, preventing rust and keeping your fleet looking professional.

> **Brand Impact:** Surface corrosion is the most visible quality signal to end-users. A jack that retains its factory finish after 2–3 seasons of road use reinforces your brand's quality positioning — while one with rusted tubes devalues the entire trailer in the resale market.

## 5. Third-Party Compliance & Certifications

For volume importers and OEMs, selling uncertified electrical components poses massive legal and liability risks.

- **The Benchmark:** Never source electrical components without independent, third-party laboratory verification.
- **The Henghong Guarantee:** As an **Intertek-certified factory**, our manufacturing processes and electrical components meet strict international safety and quality standards. We provide complete testing documentation, material specs, and quality inspection records early in the sourcing phase to ensure your product approval process is seamless.
