## 3. Step-by-Step Guide to Verifying a Manufacturer

### 3.1 Check Third-Party Verification (Intertek)

![Intertek Verified Factory](/images/company/factory-front-view.webp)

*Intertek verification confirms the manufacturer has passed a rigorous on-site factory audit.*

Third-party verification is the fastest way to confirm legitimacy. Intertek conducts on-site audits that verify:

- Actual manufacturing facilities and equipment
- Production capacity and lead times
- Quality management systems
- Workforce and technical capabilities

### 3.2 Verify Factory Ownership

Request documentation that proves the company owns its manufacturing facilities:

- Business license showing manufacturing scope
- Factory photos with identifiable landmarks
- Video calls showing the actual production floor
- Utility bills proving industrial power connections

### 3.3 Evaluate Manufacturing Capabilities

A capable RV jack manufacturer should have:

| Equipment | Purpose | Minimum Standard |
|-----------|---------|------------------|
| CNC Machines | Precision machining of components | **3+ machines** |
| Robotic Welding | Consistent weld quality | At least **1 robotic cell** |
| Assembly Lines | Efficient production | **2+ lines** |
| Testing Equipment | Quality verification | Load testers, salt-spray chambers |

### 3.4 Check Quality Control Processes

Quality control is non-negotiable for safety-critical components like RV jacks:

- **Load testing**: Every unit should be tested to **125%** of rated capacity
- **Salt-spray testing**: Corrosion resistance verification (minimum **96 hours**)
- **Visual inspection**: All units checked before packaging
- **Documentation**: Test reports, inspection records, batch traceability

### 3.5 Assess Export Experience

A manufacturer with international experience will have:

- Familiarity with US and EU safety standards
- Experience with container shipping and export documentation
- English-speaking sales and engineering staff
- References from previous international clients

## 5. Red Flags to Watch Out For

Be wary of suppliers who:

- ❌ Only show an office, not a factory
- ❌ Product images look stock or generic
- ❌ Can't provide factory audit reports
- ❌ Prices significantly lower than market average
- ❌ Won't do video calls showing production
- ❌ Lack English documentation or communication
- ❌ No experience with international shipping
