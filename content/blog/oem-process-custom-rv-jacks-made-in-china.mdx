> **TL;DR:** The OEM process for custom RV jacks takes **8-16 weeks** from initial inquiry to delivery. Key steps include specification review, tooling investment, prototype testing, quality validation, mass production, and sea freight shipping. Working with an Intertek-verified manufacturer reduces risk and ensures consistent quality.

## Why OEM from China?

Original Equipment Manufacturing (OEM) from China offers significant advantages:

- **Cost reduction**: **30-50%** savings vs. Western manufacturers
- **Customization**: Tailored specifications for your market
- **Capacity**: Scalable production from **100 to 10,000+** units
- **Quality**: Modern factories with advanced equipment

This guide walks you through the complete OEM process using a real-world example: ordering **500 units** of custom electric stabilizer jacks.

## Step 1: Initial Inquiry and Specification

### Defining Your Requirements

Before contacting manufacturers, prepare detailed specifications:

| Parameter | Example Specification |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Load Capacity | **5,000 lbs** static, **3,000 lbs** dynamic |
| Stroke Length | **14 inches** (**356 mm**) |
| Operating Voltage | **12V DC** |
| Mount Type | Bolt-on, 4-bolt pattern |
| Finish | Powder coat, black |
| Certification | CE, RoHS required |

### Requesting Quotes

When requesting quotes, ask for:

- ✅ Unit price at **100**, **500**, and **1,000** quantities
- ✅ Tooling/mold costs (one-time)
- ✅ Sample pricing and lead time
- ✅ Payment terms
- ✅ Quality certifications

## Step 3: Sample Development

### Prototyping Phase

After selecting a supplier:

#### Week 1-2: Engineering Review

- Manufacturer reviews your specifications
- Identifies any manufacturing challenges
- Suggests optimizations for producibility
- Finalizes technical drawings

#### Week 3-4: First Samples

- Initial prototypes produced
- Expect **1-3 revision cycles**
- Each revision takes **1-2 weeks**

#### Week 5-6: Testing and Approval

- Function testing
- Load testing
- Environmental testing (if required)
- Final approval

### Sample Testing Checklist

| Test | Method | Pass Criteria |
|------|--------|---------------|
| Static Load | Apply **125%** of rated capacity for **5 min** | No deformation |
| Dynamic Load | **100 cycles** at **80%** capacity | No mechanical failure |
| Drop Test | **1 meter** drop | No damage to function |
| Salt Spray | **96 hours** exposure | No red rust |
| Function | **50 extend/retract cycles** | All functions normal |

## Step 4: Tooling and Setup

### Investment Requirements

Custom RV jacks require:

#### Molds and Dies

- Worm gear molds: **$3,000-8,000**
- Housing casting dies: **$5,000-15,000**
- Sheet metal dies: **$2,000-5,000**

#### Tooling Lead Time

- Simple parts: **2-3 weeks**
- Complex castings: **4-6 weeks**
- Total tooling: **6-8 weeks**

### Cost Allocation Example (500 units)

| Item | Cost | Per Unit Impact |
|------|-------|----------------|
| Tooling | **$20,000** | **$40/unit** |
| First samples | **$2,000** | **$4/unit** |
| Mass production | **$75,000** | **$150/unit** |
| **Total** | **$97,000** | **$194/unit** |

At **500 units**, tooling adds **$40** per unit. At **2,000 units**, it's only **$10** per unit.

## Step 6: Shipping and Logistics

### Shipping Options

#### Sea Freight (Recommended for 500+ units)

- Cost: **$0.50-1.50** per kg
- Transit: **20-35 days**
- FCL (20ft container): **500-800 units**
- LCL: For smaller quantities

#### Air Freight (For samples/emergencies)

- Cost: **$3-8** per kg
- Transit: **5-7 days**
- Best for: Samples, small orders

### Shipping Checklist

- [ ] Packing list with SKU numbers
- [ ] Commercial invoice
- [ ] Certificate of origin
- [ ] Bill of lading

## Step 7: Receiving and QC

### Receiving Inspection

When your shipment arrives:

1. **Count verification**: Match to packing list
2. **Visual inspection**: Check for shipping damage
3. **Dimensional check**: Spot check critical dimensions
4. **Function test**: Sample **5%** of units
5. **Documentation**: File all certificates

### Common Issues and Solutions

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Scratches | Packing inadequate | Require foam inserts |
| Missing parts | Assembly error | **100%** check before shipping |
| Motor failure | Quality issue | **100%** function test, warranty |
| Wrong voltage | Order error | Clear labeling in order docs |
