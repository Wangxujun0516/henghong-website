## Why Trailer Jack Maintenance Matters

For RV manufacturers and fleet operators, trailer jack reliability directly affects customer satisfaction and warranty costs. A failed jack can leave an RV owner stranded or unable to set up camp.

Understanding common failure modes and implementing preventive maintenance can significantly extend jack life and reduce field failures.

## Common Problem #1: Corrosion

**Symptoms**: Rust on the outer tube, pitting on chrome surfaces, difficulty extending or retracting.

**Causes**:
- Exposure to road salt and moisture
- Lack of protective coating
- Scratches or chips in the powder coating

**Prevention**:
- Choose jacks with quality powder coating (Henghong uses salt spray-tested coating rated for 72 hours)
- Apply dielectric grease to exposed threads
- Wash jacks after exposure to road salt
- Touch up any coating chips immediately

For heavy-duty applications, consider zinc-nickel plated jacks which offer 100-hour salt spray resistance.

## Common Problem #2: Motor Failure

**Symptoms**: Jack does not extend or retract, unusual noises, tripped circuit breaker.

**Causes**:
- Overloading beyond rated capacity
- Continuous operation without cool-down
- Voltage drop due to undersized wiring
- Water ingress into the motor housing

**Prevention**:
- Never exceed the rated load capacity
- Allow motor to cool between extended operations
- Use properly sized wiring (minimum 10 AWG for 12V systems)
- Ensure motor housing seals are intact

## Common Problem #3: Bent or Damaged Jacks

**Symptoms**: Jack appears angled or crooked, binds during extension, visible bends in the tube.

**Causes**:
- Lateral (side) loads exceeding design limits
- Driving with jacks partially extended
- Impact with road debris
- Overloading

**Prevention**:
- Always fully retract jacks before driving
- Use stabilizer jacks to handle lateral loads
- Stay within rated load capacity
- Inspect jacks after any road impact

## Common Problem #4: Gear and Thread Wear

**Symptoms**: Grinding noise, rough operation, jack slips under load.

**Causes**:
- Lack of lubrication
- Contamination from dirt and debris
- Wear from normal use over time

**Prevention**:
- Lubricate gears and lead screws every 6 months
- Use a lithium-based grease for gears
- Apply white lithium grease to lead screws
- Keep jacks clean and dry when not in use

## Common Problem #5: Electrical Issues

**Symptoms**: Intermittent operation, slow speed, control panel errors.

**Causes**:
- Poor ground connection
- Corroded wiring connectors
- Low battery voltage
- Blown fuse

**Prevention**:
- Clean and tighten all electrical connections annually
- Apply dielectric grease to connectors
- Check battery voltage regularly (should be above 12.4V)
- Keep spare fuses on hand

## Preventive Maintenance Schedule

| Frequency | Task |
|-----------|------|
| Before each trip | Test full extension and retraction, check for unusual noises |
| Monthly | Visual inspection for damage, corrosion, or leaks |
| Every 3 months | Clean and lubricate moving parts |
| Every 6 months | Inspect and tighten mounting bolts |
| Annually | Full inspection: wiring, connections, seals, coating |
| As needed | Touch up coating, replace worn parts |
