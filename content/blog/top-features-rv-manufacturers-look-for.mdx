## What RV Manufacturers Prioritize in a Supplier

Choosing the right leveling system supplier is one of the most consequential decisions an RV manufacturer makes. The leveling system directly impacts end-user satisfaction, warranty costs, and brand perception.

Based on 22 years of experience supplying OEMs across North America, Europe, and Oceania, here are the top features RV manufacturers look for in a leveling system and its supplier.

## 1. Quality Certifications

The first thing procurement teams check is certification. Without the right credentials, you won't get past the initial evaluation:

- **ISO 9001:2015** — Non-negotiable quality management standard
- **Intertek or equivalent third-party verification** — Confirms factory capability
- **CE certification** — Required for European market entry
- **Product-specific testing** — Load capacity, durability, salt spray

Henghong holds ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001 certifications, and our facility is Intertek-verified through the Alibaba Gold Supplier program.

## 2. Customization Capability

No two OEM projects are identical. RV manufacturers need suppliers who can adapt:

- **Parameter adjustments**: Voltage, stroke length, load capacity
- **Appearance customization**: Logo, color, packaging, labeling
- **Material selection**: Steel, aluminum, or custom alloys
- **Design modifications**: Based on your engineering drawings or samples

With 21 R&D engineers and a provincial-level R&D center, Henghong offers full OEM/ODM customization. Our average prototype cycle is 90 days from design approval.

## 3. Manufacturing Capacity

RV manufacturers operate on tight production schedules. A supplier must demonstrate:

- **Consistent output**: Can they meet your volume requirements?
- **Scalability**: Can they ramp up when demand increases?
- **Lead time reliability**: Can they deliver on schedule?

Henghong's 39,000 m² facility with 7 production lines delivers 100,000+ units annually. Standard lead time is 45 days, with rush orders as fast as 7 days for small batches.

## 4. Quality Control Process

A supplier's QC process matters as much as their production capacity:

- **Incoming inspection**: Raw material verification
- **In-process QC**: Quality checks on every production line
- **Final inspection**: 100% testing or random sampling per client requirements
- **Traceability**: Products traceable to raw material batch
- **Closed-loop corrective action**: Documented system for handling issues

Henghong's 4-person QC team works across all production lines with documented inspection procedures and full product traceability.

## 5. Export Experience

Cross-border supply chains are complex. Experience matters:

- **Export documentation**: Correct customs paperwork
- **Shipping logistics**: Familiarity with ocean freight to major ports
- **International payment**: L/C, T/T, and other trade terms
- **After-sales support**: Warranty and technical support across time zones

With 19 years of export experience shipping from Ningbo and Shanghai ports to 40+ countries, Henghong understands international trade requirements.

## 6. Low MOQ Flexibility

Many RV manufacturers want to test products before committing to large orders:

- **Sample orders**: Test products before full production
- **Small-batch trials**: Low-volume orders for qualification
- **Scalable production**: Ability to grow order volumes over time

Henghong offers MOQ as low as 100 units with short lead times for trial orders.
